Non-Profit (NP) Membership

Designed for non-profit entities, community groups, and educational institutions with no annual fee.

  • Standard Directory Listing
  • Community Event Calendar Posting
  • Access to Networking Mixers

Cost: Free

Bronze Membership

Great for small businesses and entry-level chamber involvement.

  • Directory Listing with Website Link
  • Invitation to Monthly Business Luncheons
  • Member-to-Member Discount Eligibility

Cost: ₱2,500 / year

Silver Membership

Provides targeted exposure, advertising discounts, and promotional benefits.

  • Enhanced Directory Listing with Business Logo
  • Randomized Homepage Spotlight Exposure
  • 2 Free Tickets to Digital Transformation Seminars

Cost: ₱5,000 / year

Gold Membership

Premium tier providing maximum exposure, priority event sponsorship, and VIP access.

  • Priority Homepage Spotlight Placement
  • Full Logo Feature in All Chamber Publications
  • Free Admission to All Luncheons and Annual Galas

Cost: ₱10,000 / year
